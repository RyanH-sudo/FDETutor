<Section label="The Opening">

This is the last lesson before Volume 1. A short pact between this app and you.

</Section>

<Section label="What this app owes you">

**Honesty about pace.** I will not promise you'll be FDE-interview-ready in a weekend. I will not promise mastery in any specific window.

**Slow, complete explanations.** Every concept will be explained in full. Every term defined. Every architecture choice motivated.

**Etymology and history.** Where a word has a story, I'll tell it.

**The full picture.** I will not pretend FDE work is glamorous when it's not. We'll cover the messy parts — scope creep, customer escalations, the subtle dishonesty of optimistic timelines.

**Connection to actual work.** Every concept connects back to what an FDE will actually do — interviewing, scoping, designing, deploying, communicating, postmorteming.

</Section>

<Section label="What you owe yourself">

**Honesty about understanding.** When you don't understand something, *don't pretend you do*. Stop, re-read, run the example, ask the glossary.

**Effort on the exercises.** The retrieval is the learning. Skipping exercises turns this app into a book.

**Patience with mistakes.** Errors are the curriculum, not interruptions to it.

**Sleep on hard ideas.** When something doesn't click, close the laptop, walk, come back tomorrow.

**Coming back.** This curriculum will take time. There will be weeks you don't open the app. Show up next week.

**Talking out loud.** Read a section, close it, explain it as if teaching someone. The single most underrated learning practice.

**Practicing in the world.** Volume 12 is capstone projects you ship. But you don't have to wait. If you have an opportunity to scope or design something at work, even small, do it deliberately, write it down, reflect on what worked.

</Section>

<Section label="A note on how to feel">

Sometime in Volume 2 (probably during a customer-scoping exercise), or Volume 3 (system design under constraints), or Volume 5 (RAG depth), you'll hit a section that feels like a wall. Suddenly nothing makes sense. You'll wonder if you're cut out for this.

*Every learner has this moment.* It is universal. It is also temporary.

The strategies are: take a break, re-read the prior lesson, sleep, come back. The wall dissolves more often than it stays.

</Section>

<Section label="One ceremony">

Take a breath. Stand up if you can. Stretch.

When you sit back down and click *next*, Volume 1 begins. By the end of Volume 1 you'll know what the FDE role really is. By the end of Volume 4 you'll have command of the AI engineering stack. By the end of Volume 10 you'll be interview-prepped. By the end of Volume 12 you'll have a portfolio.

Take your time.

</Section>
